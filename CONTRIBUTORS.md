# Contributors

This project was built by the **MASSUM 2026 team** at **DSU RCSOO Nikola Karev, Strumica**, North Macedonia, for the MASSUM 2026 competition.

## Team roles

**Software and electronics**
- Raspberry Pi 5 Python program: song analysis, beat detection and LED control
- Display connections and SPI setup
- Video playback on the three displays

**Embedded systems**
- Arduino Nano program for the sliding walls
- Stepper motor control, buttons and limit switches
- Soldering the stepper control board

**Mechanical design**
- Gear systems for the motorized walls
- Fitting the stepper motors to the wall tracks

**CAD modelling**
- CAD models of the club for 3D printing: DJ booth, bar counter, tables and sliding walls
- Adapting the construction team's original design for printing

The whole team worked together on soldering, wiring, LED installation, cable management, testing and documentation.

## Original design and construction

The **construction team** at DSU RCSOO Nikola Karev, Strumica, created the original architectural club design, built the physical model, and did the interior decoration and finishing.
